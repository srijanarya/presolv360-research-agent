# Directive — grounded reasoning members

**Version 2, final.** Sections 1 to 5 are the contract as written *before* implementation, unchanged. The appendix at the end indexes what came out of it. Version 1 lives in git history.

## Objective

Prevent the reasoning stage from attaching a claim to a source unless the extraction stage actually produced that exact `(source_id, claim_text, supporting_quote)` association.

Baseline: tag `quest-baseline` (`f58b327`). Delivery branch: `quest/p2-grounded-members`.

## Allowed and forbidden

| Allowed to change | Forbidden to change |
|---|---|
| `src/research_agent/reason.py` | every other module under `src/research_agent/` |
| `tests/` | API schemas and response shapes |
| `scripts/` verification helpers | the extraction and reasoning prompt constants |

## Acceptance criteria

**Member validation**

1. Build a catalogue of complete `(source_id, claim_text, supporting_quote)` associations from the `SourceClaims` input. Union the associations when an input source identifier repeats.
2. Match source identifiers exactly. Normalize claim text and quotes with the extraction stage's existing whitespace normalization followed by lowercase, and require complete equality of both fields against the same association.
3. Reject unknown sources, unmatched pairs, missing, non-string or empty required fields, and stances outside `supports` and `contradicts`. Keep the existing confidence fallback to `medium`.
4. Preserve an accepted member's original formatting and content exactly.
5. Keep duplicate valid members. Classification already dedupes by source, so duplicates cannot manufacture corroboration.
6. Filter before adversarial prompting and before recheck eligibility. Recompute classification and derived gaps from survivors. Drop clusters left with no members.

**Adversarial revisions**

7. Count source multiplicity after validation, not before.
8. Apply a returned stance revision only when that source has exactly one surviving member in the cluster and its valid revisions agree.
9. Ignore unknown, malformed, ambiguous or conflicting revisions, keep the original stance, and log the reason.
10. Keep the existing behaviour of returning naive stances when the recheck call raises. Introduce no claim identifiers and no protocol change.

**Explicit failure**

11. Raise a descriptive `ValueError` when the response is not an object, when `clusters` is missing or not a list, or when a non-empty proposed cluster list yields no valid surviving members at all.
12. Accept an explicit empty `clusters` list as a legitimate no-claims result.
13. Skip an individual malformed cluster or member while keeping usable partial results.
14. Let the failure propagate on the existing path: reasoning to pipeline to the API's SSE `error` event. The CLI exits unsuccessfully and writes no brief.
15. Log rejection totals and reason codes using indices or validated identifiers, never raw source text.

## Tests that must exist

- Unknown source, wrong-source quote, fabricated quote, and opposite claim text on a genuine quote.
- Valid whitespace and case variants retained; shortened substrings and punctuation-transformed quotes rejected.
- Partial valid output retained; invalid stances and scalar fields rejected.
- Null, non-list and missing `clusters`; malformed member collections; all-rejected output; legitimate empty output.
- Repeated source identifiers, duplicate members, an ambiguous original, conflicting revisions, and a recheck that raises.
- Invalid members absent from recheck prompts; classifications and gaps derived from survivors.
- A reasoning failure reaching an SSE `error`, with no successful `done` and no retrievable brief.
- Two golden fixtures: an all-valid one that stays byte-identical with timestamps frozen, and an invalid one asserting exact survivors, relabeling, gaps and empty-cluster removal.

## Measurements

Regenerated by `make check` from the recorded baseline SHA, running identical fixtures against baseline and current code:

- invalid-member retention, valid-member retention, rejection counts, and model-call counts;
- the mixed fixture's drop from two model calls to one, labelled a fixture-only cost proxy;
- comparison baselines for P1, P3 and P4, regenerated without implementing them;
- production p95, monetary savings, bug-recurrence rate and DORA rates recorded as unavailable.

## Who reviews what

- **Human:** the decision record, the diff, and the measurements.
- **AI reviewers:** defects, coverage and style, on a frozen hashed snapshot, from two distinct model families.
- **Nothing merges on an AI review alone.** An unavailable review is incomplete, never approval.

## Limitations, stated rather than buried

1. Three synthetic invalid cases prove the hole existed and is closed. They are **not** a production failure rate, and recurrence of this exact bug is unproven.
2. Strict equality trades recall for trustworthiness, and the live attempts measured the price as counts on one input: attempt 1 rejected 20 of 44 (45%) proposed members; attempt 2 rejected 5 of 64 (8%) proposed members; attempt 3 rejected 20 of 66 (30%) proposed members with distinct association coverage 46 of 66 (70%). Attempts 4 and 5 failed on model-call errors before producing counts and are recorded as failed, not replaced. These are rejection counts and association coverage from identified attempts, not a semantic recall rate and not a production rate; a rewritten claim is not assumed to mean the same thing as the extracted one, and the spread between attempts is reported, not explained. Claim identifiers echoed by the model, deferred in ADR 001, are the fix.
3. A cluster's `statement` and model-surfaced gaps are still ungrounded. See ADR 001.
4. The planned pair of *free* reviewers never completed. Cross-vendor coverage came from OpenAI `gpt-5.6-terra` and DeepSeek `deepseek-flash` on frozen snapshots; the DeepSeek run cost an estimated $0.03 including one billed attempt that produced no visible output. The one pytest warning in every evidence run is the pre-existing `starlette` TestClient deprecation.
5. The P4 handoff exercise is input validation, not complete SSRF protection, and it was self-performed.
6. One production concern is disclosed and unresolved: the recheck-failure log line in `_adversarial_recheck` logs the exception text, which a provider error could fill with prompt content. Production code is frozen at `9c2cc38` for this submission; the one-line fix and its test are the first follow-up. See ADR 001 and review 6 in the review log.
